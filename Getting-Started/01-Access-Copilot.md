# 🌐 How to Access and Setup Microsoft Copilot

You can use Microsoft Copilot on different platforms for free. Here is how you can access it:

## 1. Using the Web Browser (Any Browser)
The easiest way to use Copilot is through the web.
* Go to [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com/).
* Click on **Sign in** at the top right corner.
* Log in using your Microsoft account (Outlook, Hotmail, Live).

## 2. Using Microsoft Edge Browser
Copilot is built directly into Microsoft Edge!
* Open the **Microsoft Edge** browser.
* Look for the **Copilot icon** (a colorful loop) in the top right corner or the sidebar.
* Click it, and a chat window will open on the right side of your screen.

## 3. Using the Mobile App
You can use Copilot on your phone too!
* Go to the **Google Play Store** (Android) or **Apple App Store** (iOS).
* Search for **"Microsoft Copilot"** and download the app.
* Open the app, sign in, and you can even use voice commands or take photos to ask questions!
