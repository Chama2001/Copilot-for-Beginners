# 🕰️ The History of Microsoft Copilot

Understanding how Copilot evolved helps us see the future of AI at Microsoft.

## Timeline of Evolution
* **February 2023 (Bing Chat):** Microsoft launched "Bing Chat", integrating OpenAI's GPT-4 technology directly into the Bing search engine.
* **March 2023 (M365 Integration):** Microsoft announced "Microsoft 365 Copilot", bringing generative AI to everyday apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
* **November 2023 (The Rebranding):** Microsoft officially rebranded Bing Chat to **"Microsoft Copilot"** to create a single, unified AI experience across all their products.
* **January 2024 (Copilot Pro):** Launched Copilot Pro, a premium subscription offering individuals faster performance and priority access to the latest AI models.
* **Present:** Copilot is now deeply integrated into Windows, the Edge browser, mobile apps, and developer tools like GitHub Copilot and Copilot Studio.
