# 🤖 Building Custom AI Agents with Copilot Studio

Did you know you can build your own version of Copilot? **Microsoft Copilot Studio** allows anyone to create custom AI agents without writing complex code!

## What is a Custom AI Agent?
A standard AI answers general questions. A **Custom AI Agent** answers questions based on *your specific data*. 
For example, you can upload your company's HR manual or your school notes, and the AI will answer questions strictly based on those documents. (This uses a technology called **Retrieval-Augmented Generation or RAG**).

## How to Build Your First Agent (Basic Steps):
1. **Access the Studio:** Go to [copilotstudio.microsoft.com](https://copilotstudio.microsoft.com/) and sign in.
2. **Create a New Copilot:** Click on "New Copilot" and give it a name (e.g., "Study Buddy").
3. **Add Knowledge:** This is the most important step! Upload your PDF documents, Word files, or provide website links that you want your AI to learn from.
4. **Define Instructions:** Tell the AI how to behave. (e.g., *"You are a helpful assistant for university students. Always answer in a polite and encouraging tone."*)
5. **Test and Publish:** Use the built-in chat window to test your AI. Once you are happy with it, you can publish it to Microsoft Teams, a website, or Facebook Messenger!

## Why is this useful?
* **For Businesses:** Customer support bots that know all product details.
* **For Students:** A personal tutor that only asks questions from your syllabus.
