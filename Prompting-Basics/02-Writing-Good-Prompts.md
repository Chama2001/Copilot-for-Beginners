# ✍️ How to Write Good Prompts (For Beginners)

A **Prompt** is the message or instruction you type into Copilot. To get the best answers, you need to write clear prompts.

## The Simple Formula
**Context + Task + Format = Great Answer**

### ❌ Bad Example:
> "Write an email for leave." *(Too short, no details)*

### ✅ Good Example:
> "Act as a software engineer [Context]. Write a professional email to my manager asking for 2 days of sick leave [Task]. Keep it short and polite [Format]."

## Examples to Try:
1. **Summarize:** "Summarize the history of Artificial Intelligence in 3 bullet points."
2. **Translate:** "Translate 'Happy New Year' into 5 different languages."
3. **Brainstorm:** "Give me 5 creative naming ideas for a new tech startup."
