# 📈 Business Planning & Strategy

Copilot can act as your virtual business partner, helping you brainstorm, plan, and strategize for your enterprise.

## 1. Writing Business Proposals
* **Prompt:** *"Write a comprehensive business proposal for a new tech enterprise focused on software development and AI solutions. Include an executive summary, target market analysis, and revenue models."*

## 2. Market Research
* **Prompt:** *"What are the current global trends in digital e-book publishing and online tech education platforms? Summarize the top 5 key insights."*

## 3. Naming and Branding
