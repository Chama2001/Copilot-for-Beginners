# AI Ethics and Safety Guidelines

When using Microsoft Copilot or any Generative AI, it is crucial to build and learn responsibly.

## Key Principles
* **Data Privacy:** Never share sensitive, personal, or confidential company data in your prompts.
* **Verify Everything (Hallucinations):** AI can confidently give incorrect information. Always double-check facts, especially for code and business logic.
* **Bias and Fairness:** Be aware that AI models can inherit human biases. Review generated content to ensure it is inclusive.
* **Transparency:** Always disclose when content, code, or images have been generated or heavily modified by AI.
