# 📝 Microsoft Copilot Cheat Sheet

Here is a quick reference guide with the best prompt formulas for different tasks. Bookmark this page for your daily work!

## 👩‍💻 For Developers & Tech Enthusiasts
* **Code Generation:** "Write a Python function using Flask to connect to a SQLite database."
* **Web Dev:** "Create a responsive hero section in Next.js using Tailwind CSS."
* **Debugging:** "Explain this error message and suggest a fix: [Paste Error]"
* **Tech Concepts:** "Explain the concept of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) in simple terms."

## 📢 For Public Relations & Marketing
* **Event Promotion:** "Write a catchy social media caption for an upcoming public speaking event. Include 3 relevant hashtags."
* **Professional Emails:** "Draft a polite and enthusiastic email inviting a guest speaker to our club meeting."
* **Image Generation:** "Create a high-quality, modern flyer background for a tech workshop, abstract tech elements, blue and purple colors."

## 🎓 For Students & Researchers
* **Summarization:** "Summarize the key findings of this research paper in 5 bullet points: [Paste abstract]"
* **Study Plan:** "Create a 7-day study schedule for an upcoming university exam on Artificial Intelligence."
* **Proofreading:** "Check this paragraph for grammar and improve its professional tone: [Paste text]"

## 💡 Universal Prompt Formula
Remember the golden rule for prompting:
**Context (Who are you?) + Task (What do you want?) + Format (How should it look?) = Great Result**
