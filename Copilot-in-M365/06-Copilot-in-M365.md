# 📊 Using Copilot in Microsoft 365

Copilot is integrated directly into Microsoft 365 apps to help you work faster and smarter.

## 1. Copilot in Microsoft Word
You can generate entire document drafts, summarize long reports, or rewrite sections to sound more professional.
* **Try this:** Open a blank Word document, click the Copilot icon, and type: *"Write a 2-page project proposal for a new AI-based healthcare app."*

## 2. Copilot in PowerPoint
Turn plain text or Word documents into beautiful presentations instantly.
* **Try this:** Open PowerPoint, click Copilot, and type: *"Create a 5-slide presentation about the benefits of learning Python, including speaker notes and images."*

## 3. Copilot in Excel
Analyze data, create charts, and write formulas without knowing complex math.
* **Try this:** Select your data table, open Copilot, and ask: *"Highlight the top 3 highest sales in this dataset"* or *"Create a bar chart showing monthly revenue."*
